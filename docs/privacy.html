Privacy policy

Niaromi is an Android reading app. Your library and reading progress are stored on your device. You can use the reader without connecting a Google account.

Google sign-in and Drive backups

If you connect Google Drive, Niaromi uses your Google account information to identify the connected account and uses your authorization to create, list, download, and restore Niaromi backups in your Drive. The app requests the Drive app-data and file-specific permissions for this feature. A backup can include your library, reading progress, and settings, depending on the backup options you select.

The app communicates with Google Drive directly. Niaromi does not send your Google account information, Drive authorization, or backup contents to a Niaromi server. Niaromi does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

You can disconnect Google Drive in Niaromi or revoke Niaromi’s access in your Google Account connections. Revoking access does not itself delete backups already stored in your Google account.

Extensions and diagnostics

When you use extensions, the third-party sources they connect to can receive requests such as searches and chapter requests. Their own privacy practices apply. Niaromi does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. If you choose to share a crash log, review it before posting publicly.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the maintainer through the project’s GitHub maintainer profile. Avoid posting private information in public issues.
