Niaromi

An Android reader for manga, manhwa, comics, and EPUB books. Organize your library by genre or collection, pick up where you left off, and download chapters for offline reading.

Download for Android

Back up your library

Create a local backup or connect Google Drive to save and restore backups in your Google account. Google sign-in is optional and is used for Drive backups.

Source and support

Niaromi is an open-source variant of Mihon. It does not host reading content. View the source code or report a problem.
