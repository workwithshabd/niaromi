Make it yours

Organize titles by genre or custom collection, sort by last read, and see recent chapters right on Home.

Find your next read

Search across installed extensions from Explore and prioritize the sources you use most.

Read offline

Download chapters for later, import EPUBs, and come back to the page where you stopped.

Keep a backup

Save your library and reading progress locally or connect Google Drive for optional cloud backups.
