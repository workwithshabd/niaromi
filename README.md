# Niaromi

### Full-featured manga reader for Android with Cloud Sync
Discover and read manga, webtoons, comics, and more – easier than ever on your Android device. Featuring real-time Google Sign-In and Google Drive Cloud Backup & Sync.

[![CI](https://img.shields.io/github/actions/workflow/status/workwithshabd/niaromi/build.yml?labelColor=27303D)](https://github.com/workwithshabd/niaromi/actions)
[![License: Apache-2.0](https://img.shields.io/github/license/workwithshabd/niaromi?labelColor=27303D&color=0877d2)](/LICENSE)

## Features

<div align="left">

* **Google Drive Cloud Sync**: Sign in with Google to automatically back up and sync your entire library, read chapters, bookmarks, categories, history, tracking, and app preferences. Seamlessly restore on login or device change.
* **Local & Online Reading**: Extensive support for local reading as well as third-party extensions.
* **Configurable Reader**: Multiple reading modes (Left-to-Right, Right-to-Left, Vertical, Webtoon, Continuous), color filters, dual-page mode, and transition controls.
* **Tracker Integration**: Track your reading progress with MangaBaka, MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu, MangaUpdates, Shikimori, Bangumi, and Hikka.
* **Library Management**: Custom categories, automatic background library updates, and smart search.
* **Themes & UI**: Modern Material 3 design with light, dark, and OLED black themes, dynamic colors, and tablet support.

</div>
